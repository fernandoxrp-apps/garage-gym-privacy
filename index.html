Garage Gym — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Garage Gym is a personal workout, running and supplement tracker built for a single user. It is not distributed on the App Store and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

The short version: there is no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no user accounts, and the developer has no ability to see your data.

What the app stores, and where

Data Where it lives
Workout logs (exercises, sets, weights, repetitions), session durations, runs, supplement and peptide schedules and their daily check-off, manually entered body weight, sleep, resting heart rate and HRV On-device application storage only. Never transmitted anywhere.
WHOOP access token, refresh token, and the WHOOP API client ID and secret you supply The iOS Keychain, via the operating system's secure storage.
Step counts Read from the phone's motion coprocessor (Core Motion) and stored on the device. Never transmitted.

WHOOP

If you choose to connect a WHOOP account, the app requests read-only access to the following, using WHOOP's standard OAuth 2.0 authorisation:

  • Physiological cycles (day strain, energy expenditure)
  • Recovery scores, heart rate variability and resting heart rate
  • Sleep
  • Workouts
  • Profile and body measurements

These requests are made directly from your phone to WHOOP's servers at api.prod.whoop.com. There is no intermediary server, because the developer does not operate one. The retrieved data is stored on the device alongside your other training data and is not forwarded anywhere.

The app never requests write access and cannot modify anything in your WHOOP account. You can revoke access at any time from within the app, or from your WHOOP account settings.

Third parties

There is exactly one, and it is worth being explicit about:

YouTube. Exercise technique videos are embedded from YouTube. When you play one, your device contacts Google's servers directly, and Google will receive your IP address and may set cookies, exactly as if you had opened the video in a browser. This is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one. If you never open a video, no such request is made.

Beyond YouTube and WHOOP — the latter only if you connect it — the app makes no network requests at all.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting
  • No user accounts, sign-up or login
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data — there is no data to share, as none of it reaches the developer
  • No location tracking
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone or camera

Health information

Some of what the app records — training history, sleep, heart rate, body weight and a supplement schedule — is health-related and personal. It is treated the same way as everything else: stored locally, transmitted nowhere.

The app records what you enter. It does not provide medical advice, and it does not suggest, validate or check any dosage.

Deleting your data

  • All of it: delete the app from your phone. Because everything is local, nothing survives.
  • From inside the app: History → Reset all data.
  • WHOOP data only: disconnecting WHOOP offers to remove everything imported from it. Disconnecting also discards the stored tokens.

Children

The app is a personal tool and is not directed at or intended for children.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects any of the above, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.
